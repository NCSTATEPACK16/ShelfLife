Shelf Life · Design System v0.1

Warm grey fixtures.
Saturated products.
Phone first.

The token system and screen layouts for a supermarket tycoon sim shipping to web and iOS from one codebase. Every colour and measure here lives in content/design/tokens.json — the single source of truth for the game UI and the landing page.

Compact (390×844) is the primary layout. Regular is the expansion.

01 · COLOUR

Two families, never mixed

Fixture is the desaturated warm-grey ramp the store world is built from. It is also the entire UI chrome. Product is the saturated family, reserved for goods, data series, and state. Chrome never borrows a product colour, which is what lets a full shelf read as full from zoomed out.

Semantic ≠ accent. Price Red is the brand accent and the critical state. Where both could apply in one view, the state wins and the accent steps back to Fixture 600 — a red "Buy" button next to a red overdraft warning teaches nothing.

02 · TYPE

Signage, body, register

Three roles, drawn from what a supermarket actually looks like. Wayfinding signage is heavy and tight. Shelf tags and receipts are monospaced — so every number in this game is monospaced, with tabular figures, and every uppercase label is too.

03 · HUD

The in-game screen

The canvas runs full-bleed under the notch and home indicator; every control sits inside the safe-area insets. On compact the HUD is a top status strip plus a bottom action bar — nothing floats, because a floating window on a 390 px screen is a modal in denial.

What changes between breakpoints — and what doesn't. Regular gains a docked right rail of live stat tiles and a sixth action. It gains no new capability. Anything you can only do at Regular is a bug, because the iOS build is the same build.

05 · TELLS

Every formula term has a face

The simulation is a multinomial logit model with per-household loyalty. The player never sees one line of that. They see a shopper put the milk back. A satisfaction or impulse term with no declared visual tell fails content validation in CI — the full table lives in docs/design/gentle-surface.md.

Silence is a feature. Tells fire only above a magnitude threshold; one bubble per shopper (highest magnitude wins); and the screen caps at 8 simultaneous bubbles on Regular, 4 on Compact. A store where nothing is wrong should look calm. Calm is the reward.

06 · RULES

Non-negotiables

  • 7.1All input is a semantic intent — tap, drag, pinch, longpress. No raw pointer listeners outside src/platform/input.
  • 7.1Hover is never load-bearing. Anything discoverable only by hovering does not exist on a phone.
  • 7.2Every panel declares its compact form. A panel that renders only at Regular fails review.
  • 7.3Nothing interactive in the bottom 34 px — the home-indicator swipe wins that fight.
  • —44 px minimum hit target, at both breakpoints, no exceptions.
  • 11.6No hardcoded hex in src/ui or the landing page. Tokens only; CI enforces it.
  • 12.4No bare numbers. Every KPI carries a benchmark or a trailing comparison.
