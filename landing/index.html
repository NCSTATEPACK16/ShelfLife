A 16-bit supermarket management sim. You run one store against a ladder of ten parody grocery chains — and difficulty isn't measured in store size. It's measured in how much people love the competition.

Play in browser Web build · iOS coming

The final boss is a corner store

Every tycoon game makes you fight a bigger competitor. This one makes you fight a better-liked one. Money can beat scale. Money cannot beat a chain where the cashier knows your kid's name.

Community Love — a loyalty-resilience score money can't attack directly.

Where you put the milk is a revenue decision

Shoppers walk real paths through your floor plan, and everything they pass is a chance to buy something that wasn't on the list. Put the dairy on the back wall and impulse revenue goes up — at the cost of every shopper who came in for one thing and wanted out.

You lost because of a decision, not a dice roll

Same seed, same outcome. Every dollar traces back to a visible cause, and every number on the finance screen drills down to the events that produced it.

All chains, brands, and products in this game are fictional parodies. Any resemblance to actual retailers is satirical.
